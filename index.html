Blending tradition & innovation to create unforgettable dining experiences

Situated in the heart of Trans Amadi, Port Harcourt, Antoinette Gardens and Guest House Services is an oasis away from the city rush. Founded by Anthony & Ebiakpo Okudu, we bring together the finest Eastern Nigerian culinary mastery—from authentic, slow-simmered spicy Isi Ewu and Nkwobi to fresh barbecue catfish—served under tranquil garden canopies with chilled drinks and live music.
